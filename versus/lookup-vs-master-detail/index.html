Lookup vs master-detail vs junction

Two objects need to be related. Is this a loose pointer, an owned child, or a many-to-many?

LookupMaster-detailJunction object

The matrix

Find your situation in the left column. Green means a committed answer; amber means the honest answer is a trade-off — the note says on what.

Your situationPickWhy
Child should survive if the parent is deletedLookupLookups are independent records with an optional pointer. Master-detail cascade-deletes the children — that is the feature, not a setting you can later regret your way out of.
Need a roll-up summary (count, sum, min, max) on the parentMaster-detailRoll-up summaries require master-detail. Lookup roll-ups exist as AppExchange or Flow/Apex workarounds; they are not the same feature and they will surprise you at volume.
Child should inherit the parent's sharingMaster-detailDetail records have no owner of their own — they ride the master's sharing. If the child needs independent OWD, teams, or queues, it is a lookup.
Relationship is optional, or the 'parent' might not exist yetLookupMaster-detail is required. You cannot insert a detail without its master, and you cannot convert a populated lookup into master-detail without first backfilling every row.
Many-to-many (a Contact on many Campaigns, a Product in many Bundles)Junction objectA custom object with two master-detail fields. The junction is the relationship. Do not fake this with two lookups unless you truly need independent ownership and no roll-ups.
Reparenting children to a different parent laterLookupMaster-detail reparenting is off by default and, even when enabled, is a sharp edge. Lookups reparent freely.
Already at two master-detail fields on the childLookupHard platform limit: two master-detail relationships per object. The third 'must-have' is a lookup, or you redesign.

Rule of thumb

  • Default to lookup. Master-detail is the exception you earn: cascade delete, roll-ups, or inherited sharing.
  • If you need roll-ups *and* independent ownership, you do not get both from the data model. Pick, or compute the roll-up in automation.
  • Many-to-many is a junction object with two master-details, not two lookups named left and right.
  • You can convert lookup → master-detail later only if every child already has a parent and you are under the two-MD cap. Design as if you cannot.
  • Changing this after data exists is a migration, not a Setup checkbox. Get it right on the first object you create.
